TMUA-Like Practice Batch 2

Instructions

  • Time: 60 minutes
  • Do not pause between questions unless needed
  • Mark each answer with confidence: high / medium / low
  • After finishing, classify each question as: routine, moderate, or hard

Question 1

Find the values of x satisfying $$ |x-3| = 5 $$

Question 2

Solve the simultaneous equations: $$ 2x+y=8 $$ $$ x-y=1 $$

Question 3

If $$ f(x) = x^2 - 6x + 8, $$ find the set of values of $x$ for which $f(x) \ge 0$.

Question 4

Differentiate $$ y = \frac{x^2+1}{x} $$

Question 5

Evaluate $$ $int_0^1 (3x^2 + 2x)\$,dx $$

Question 6

Solve $$ $\sin 2x = \$frac{$\sqrt{2}}{2}, \$quad 0$\le x \$\le \\pi $$

Question 7

Find the gradient of the tangent to $$ y = x^3 - 3x + 1 $$

at the point where $x=2$.

Question 8

A committee of 4 must be chosen from 9 students. How many possible committees are there?

Question 9

A bag contains 2 red, 3 blue, and 5 green balls. Find the probability of drawing a blue or green ball.

Question 10

The line through $(1,1)$ and $(5,9)$ has equation $$ y=mx+c. $$ Find m and c.

Question 11

If the sum of the first n positive integers is 120, find n.

Question 12

Prove that for any integer n, if n is even then $n^2$ is even.

Review checklist

  • Did any question rely on a weak method?
  • Which steps were slow but correct?
  • Which results were guessed?
  • Write 3 recurring mistakes to target before the next batch.
