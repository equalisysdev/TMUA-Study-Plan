TMUA Full Mock Paper 1

Instructions

  • Time: 2 hours
  • There are 20 questions in this mock paper
  • Attempt every question
  • Keep a strict timer
  • Score each question as correct, partially correct, or incorrect

Section A: Algebra and functions

1.

Solve $$ 3x^2 - 5x - 2 = 0 $$

2.

Find the range of $$ y = x^2 - 4x + 1 $$

3.

Solve $$ \frac{x+1}{x-2} = 3 $$

4.

If $f(x) = 2x+1$, find $f^{-1}(x)$.

5.

Solve the inequality $$ x^2 - 9 < 0 $$

Section B: Calculus

6.

Differentiate $$ y = (x^2+1)(x-3) $$

7.

Find the stationary points of $$ y = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x $$

8.

Evaluate $$ $int_0^2 (x^2 + x)\$,dx $$

9.

Find the area between the curve $y=x^2$ and the line $y=x$ from $x=0$ to $x=1$.

10.

A particle’s displacement is $$ s(t)=t^2-4t+3. $$ When is it at rest?

Section C: Trigonometry and geometry

11.

Solve $$ $\cos x = \$frac{1}{2}, $quad 0\$\le x $\le 2\$\pi $$

12.

Find the equation of the line through $$ (3,2) $ and \$$ (7,10) $.

13.

A triangle has sides 7, 7, and 10. Find its area.

14.

What is the radius of the circle with equation $$ x^2 + y^2 = 25 $$

15.

Simplify $$ $frac{\$\sin^2 x}{1-\\cos^2 x} $$

Section D: Proof and combinatorics

16.

Prove that the product of two odd integers is odd.

17.

How many ways can 6 students be seated in a row?

18.

A fair coin is tossed 4 times. Find the probability of exactly 2 heads.

19.

From a group of 10 people, how many ways can a president and secretary be chosen?

20.

A bag contains 3 green and 2 red balls. Find the probability of drawing green then red without replacement.

Marking and review strategy

  • Score 1 point per correct answer
  • Record how long each question took
  • Identify whether errors were from method, arithmetic, or time pressure
  • After the mock, create a repair list with at least 5 tasks
