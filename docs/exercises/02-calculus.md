# Calculus Drills

## Learning objectives
- differentiate standard functions accurately
- interpret derivatives in context
- apply integration to area and simple accumulation
- recognise maxima, minima, and stationary points

## Exercise Set A: Differentiation

### 1. Differentiate
$$
 f(x) = x^4 - 3x^3 + 2x - 7
$$

### 2. Differentiate
$$
 g(x) = x^2 e^x
$$

### 3. Differentiate
$$
 h(x)=\frac{x^2+1}{x}
$$

### 4. Find the gradient of the curve
$$
 y = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x
$$

at the point where $x=2$.

### 5. Find the stationary points of
$$
 y = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2
$$

and classify them.

## Exercise Set B: Integration

### 6. Integrate
$$
$int (3x^2 - 4x + 5)\$,dx
$$

### 7. Integrate
$$
$int (2x+1)^4\$,dx
$$

### 8. Evaluate
$$
$int_0^2 (x^2 + 3x)\$,dx
$$

### 9. Find the area enclosed by
$$
 y=x^2 $quad \$text{and} \quad y=4
$$

### 10. If
$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=6x^2-4x
$$

and $y=5$ when $x=1$, find $y$.

## Exercise Set C: Graph interpretation

### 11. A function is increasing where its derivative is positive. Find the intervals where
$$
 f(x)=x^3-6x^2+9x+1
$$

is increasing.

### 12. A curve has equation
$$
 y=2x^3-9x^2+12x-5
$$

Find the x-values where the tangent is horizontal.

### 13. A particle moves so that its displacement is
$$
 s(t)=t^3-6t^2+9t
$$

Find when the particle is instantaneously at rest.

### 14. A curve has equation
$$
 y = x^3 - 3x + 1
$$

Find the local maximum and minimum points.

### 15. The area between a curve and the x-axis from x=0 to x=3 is required. Sketch and determine whether a sign check is necessary.

## Technique focus
- Product rule and quotient rule must be used cleanly
- For tangents and stationary points, differentiate first
- For area problems, always sketch if necessary
- For integration, re-check constants and bounds

## Answer notes
- Question 2: use product rule
- Question 3: rewrite as $x + x^{-1}$ before differentiating
- Question 5: solve derivative equals zero and test sign changes
- Question 8: integrate and substitute upper lower bound
- Question 9: find intersections first, then integrate difference
- Questions 11–14: use derivative and second derivative or sign analysis
