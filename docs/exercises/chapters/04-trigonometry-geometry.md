# Chapter 4: Trigonometry and Geometry

Give exact answers where possible. Draw a diagram before using a geometric formula.

## Foundation
1. Find `sin(pi/6)`, `cos(pi/3)`, and `tan(pi/4)`.
2. Simplify `sin^2(x)+cos^2(x)`.
3. Solve `sin(x)=1/2` on `[0,2pi]`.
4. Solve `cos(x)=-1/2` on `[0,2pi]`.
5. Simplify `sin(x)/cos(x)`.
6. Find the gradient of the line through `(1,2)` and `(5,10)`.
7. Find the midpoint of `(-2,7)` and `(4,-1)`.
8. Find the distance between `(1,2)` and `(4,6)`.
9. Find the equation of the circle centred at the origin through `(3,4)`.
10. Find the area of a triangle with base 8 and height 5.

## Core technique
11. Prove `1+tan^2(x)=sec^2(x)`.
12. Solve `2sin(x)-sqrt(3)=0` on `[0,2pi]`.
13. Solve `sin(2x)=0` on `[0,pi]`.
14. Simplify `(1-cos^2(x))/sin(x)` where defined.
15. Find the intersection of `y=2x+1` and `y=x^2-3`.
16. Find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining `(0,0)` and `(6,2)`.
17. Find the angle between lines with slopes 1 and -1.
18. Use the cosine rule to find the third side with sides 5, 7 and included angle 60 degrees.
19. Find the area of a triangle with sides 5,5,8.
20. Find the radius and centre of `x^2+y^2-6x+4y-3=0`.

## TMUA challenge
21. Solve `cos(2x)=1/2` on `[0,pi]`.
22. Show that `sin(x)+sin(3x)=2sin(2x)cos(x)`.
23. Find the maximum of `3sin(x)+4cos(x)`.
24. Find all intersections of the circle `x^2+y^2=25` and line `y=3`.
25. A triangle has sides 7, 8, and 9. Find its area using Heron's formula.
26. Find the equation of the tangent to `x^2+y^2=25` at `(3,4)`.
27. A regular hexagon has side length 2. Find its area.
28. Prove that the angle subtended by a diameter at the circumference is a right angle.
29. Find the exact area of the region between `y=sin(x)` and the x-axis from `0` to `pi`.
30. Solve `sin(x)=cos(2x)` on `[0,2pi]` and check every candidate.
