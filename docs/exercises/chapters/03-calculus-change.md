# Chapter 3: Calculus and Change

Use exact values. Sketch curves and derivative sign charts where they clarify the argument.

## Foundation
1. Differentiate $x^5-3x^2+7$.
2. Differentiate $4x^3-1/x$.
3. Differentiate $(x+1)(x^2-2)$.
4. Differentiate $x^2 e^x$.
5. Find the gradient of $y=x^3-4x$ at $x=2$.
6. Find the stationary points of $y=x^3-3x$.
7. Integrate $6x^2-4x+1$.
8. Integrate `1/x`.
9. Evaluate $integral_0^2 (3x^2+1) dx$.
10. Find the tangent to $y=x^2+3x$ at $x=1$.

## Core technique
11. Find and classify the stationary points of $x^3-6x^2+9x+2$.
12. Find where $f(x)=x^3-3x^2-9x$ is increasing.
13. Find the area between $y=x$ and $y=x^2$ from `0` to `1`.
14. Evaluate $integral_1^e (1/x) dx$.
15. Find `y` if $dy/dx=4x-3$ and $y=2$ when $x=1$.
16. Differentiate $(2x+1)^5$.
17. Differentiate $ln(x^2+1)$.
18. Find the normal to $y=x^2$ at `(1,1)`.
19. Find the x-coordinate of the maximum of `x(10-x)`.
20. A particle has $s(t)=t^3-6t^2+9t$; find when it is at rest.

## TMUA challenge
21. Find the area enclosed by $y=4-x^2$ and the x-axis.
22. Find the minimum of `x+4/x` for $x>0$ using calculus.
23. A curve has derivative $3x^2-12x+9$. Find intervals of increase and decrease.
24. Find all tangents to $y=x^2$ passing through `(0,-1)`.
25. Evaluate $integral_0^1 x ln(x) dx$ by integration by parts.
26. Find the stationary points of $y=x^4-4x^2$ and classify them.
27. A rectangle under $y=12-x^2$ has one side on the x-axis. Find its maximum area.
28. Find the number of roots of $x^3-3x+1=0$ using the derivative and graph shape.
29. If $f'(x)=2x/(x^2+1)$ and $f(0)=4$, find `f(x)`.
30. Prove that $x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}\ge 2$ for positive $x$, then identify when equality occurs.
