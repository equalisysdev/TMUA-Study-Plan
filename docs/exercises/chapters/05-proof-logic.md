# Chapter 5: Proof and Logic

Write complete arguments. A numerical check is evidence, not a proof.

## Foundation
1. Prove that the sum of two even integers is even.
2. Prove that the product of two odd integers is odd.
3. Prove that the square of an odd integer is odd.
4. Prove that if `n` is divisible by 6, it is divisible by 3.
5. Give a counterexample to the claim: every prime is odd.
6. Give a counterexample to the claim: if `ab` is even then both `a` and `b` are even.
7. Rewrite the converse of: if `n` is divisible by 4 then n is even.
8. Decide whether the statement in question 7 is true and justify it.
9. Prove that the sum of the first two odd numbers is 4.
10. Explain why checking the first 100 cases cannot prove a statement for all integers.

## Core technique
11. Prove by induction that $1+2+...+n=n(n+1)/2$.
12. Prove by induction that $1+3+...+(2n-1)=n^2$.
13. Prove by induction that $2^n>n$ for all integers $nge 1$.
14. Prove by contradiction that \\sqrt{2} is irrational.
15. Prove by contradiction that there is no largest integer.
16. Prove the contrapositive of: if $n^2$ is odd then n is odd.
17. Decide whether $n^2+n$ is always even and prove your conclusion.
18. Prove that the sum of three consecutive integers is divisible by 3.
19. Find a counterexample to: $x^2>x$ for every real x.
20. Translate “at least one of A and B is true” into logical notation.

## TMUA challenge
21. Prove that among any three consecutive integers one is divisible by 3.
22. Prove that the product of four consecutive integers is divisible by 24.
23. Prove that there are infinitely many primes.
24. Determine whether the product of two rational numbers must be rational, and prove it.
25. If `a+b` is irrational, must at least one of `a,b` be irrational? Give a precise proof.
26. Prove that a strictly increasing function is one-to-one.
27. Disprove: every bounded sequence is convergent.
28. Prove that if `x+y` and `x-y` are even integers, then x and y are integers of the same parity.
29. Find all positive integers n for which $n^2+1$ is divisible by `n+1`.
30. Analyse the claim: if `p` is prime and `p|ab`, then `p|a` or `p|b`. State the theorem and give a proof.
