# Chapter 6: Combinatorics and Probability

State whether order matters before choosing a counting method.

## Foundation
1. How many ways can 5 books be arranged on a shelf?
2. How many committees of 3 can be chosen from 8 people?
3. How many 3-digit numbers use distinct digits from 1,2,3,4,5?
4. How many outcomes are possible when two dice are rolled?
5. Find the probability of rolling a 6 on a fair die.
6. Find the probability of exactly two heads in three fair coin tosses.
7. Find the probability of a total of 7 with two fair dice.
8. How many subsets does a set of 5 elements have?
9. Find the coefficient of $x^2$ in $(1+x)^5$.
10. In how many ways can a captain and vice-captain be chosen from 10 people?

## Core technique
11. How many arrangements of the letters in MATH are possible?
12. How many arrangements of A,B,C,D use A before B?
13. How many 4-digit even numbers can be made from 1,2,3,4,5 without repetition?
14. Find the probability of drawing two red cards from a standard deck without replacement.
15. A bag has 4 red and 6 blue balls. Find the probability of two balls of different colours without replacement.
16. A fair die is rolled twice. Find the probability that the second score exceeds the first.
17. Find the expected score of a fair six-sided die.
18. Find the coefficient of $x^3$ in $(2+x)^5$.
19. How many solutions in nonnegative integers satisfy $a+b+c=7$?
20. How many paths move from `(0,0)` to `(3,2)` using only right and up steps?

## TMUA challenge
21. How many arrangements of the letters in BANANA are distinct?
22. How many committees of 4 from 10 include two specified people and exclude a third?
23. A family has 4 children. Find the probability of exactly two girls, assuming equal independent probabilities.
24. Three balls are drawn from 5 red and 4 blue. Find the probability of exactly two red balls.
25. Two dice are rolled. Find the probability that the product is a multiple of 6.
26. Find the expected number of heads in 8 fair coin tosses.
27. How many positive integer solutions satisfy $x+y+z=10$?
28. In how many ways can 6 people sit around a circular table?
29. A test has 8 questions, each with 4 choices. How many answer patterns contain exactly two answers of choice A?
30. A random permutation of 1,2,3,4,5 is selected. Find the probability that no number is in its original position.
