# Corrections: Algebra and Equations

## Foundation

### 1. Expand
$$(2x-3)(x+5)=2x^2+10x-3x-15=\boxed{2x^2+7x-15}.$$

### 2. Factorise
$$x^2-9x+20=(x-4)(x-5).$$

### 3. Simplify
$$\frac{x^2-16}{x^2-8x+16}=\frac{(x-4)(x+4)}{(x-4)^2}=\boxed{\frac{x+4}{x-4}},$$ with $x\ne4$.

### 4. Solve
$$5x-7=3x+9\implies 2x=16\implies \boxed{x=8}.$$

### 5. Solve
$$(x-3)(x-4)=0,$$ so $\boxed{x=3,4}$.

### 6. Solve
$$2x^2+x-6=(2x-3)(x+2),$$ hence $\boxed{x=\frac32,-2}$.

### 7. Solve
The product is positive outside the roots $-1$ and $4$: $\boxed{x<-1\text{ or }x>4}$.

### 8. Solve
$$|3x-2|\le10\iff -10\le3x-2\le10,$$ giving $\boxed{-\frac83\le x\le4}$.

### 9. Remainder
By the remainder theorem, the remainder is $p(2)=2(2)^3-5(2)+4=\boxed{10}$.

### 10. Find $a^2+b^2$
$$a^2+b^2=(a+b)^2-2ab=81-28=\boxed{53}.$$

## Core technique

### 11. Solve
Restrictions: $x\ne\pm1$. Multiplying by $(x-1)(x+1)$ gives $2x=x^2-1$, so $\boxed{x=1\pm\sqrt2}$.

### 12. Two distinct real roots
$$\Delta=(-6)^2-4k=36-4k>0,$$ so $\boxed{k<9}$.

### 13. Solve
$$(x-2)(x-3)\le0,$$ therefore $\boxed{2\le x\le3}$.

### 14. Find the expression
$$\left(x+\frac1x\right)^2=x^2+2+\frac1{x^2}=25,$$ so $\boxed{x^2+\frac1{x^2}=23}$.

### 15. Factorise
Let $u=x^2$. Then $u^2-5u+4=(u-1)(u-4)$, hence $\boxed{(x-1)(x+1)(x-2)(x+2)}$.

### 16. Solve
Squaring gives $x+4=(x-2)^2$, so $x^2-5x=0$. Checking $x\ge2$ leaves $\boxed{x=5}$.

### 17. Solve
$$2^{x+1}=16=2^4\implies\boxed{x=3}.$$

### 18. Solve
$$\log_2(x-1)=3\implies x-1=8\implies\boxed{x=9}.$$

### 19. Coefficient
The $x^2$ term is $\binom52(-2)^2x^2=\boxed{40x^2}$, so the coefficient is $\boxed{40}$.

### 20. Simplify
For $x>0$, $\sqrt{x}=x^{1/2}$, so $\boxed{x}$.

## TMUA challenge

### 21. Minimum
$$x^2-8x+19=(x-4)^2+3,$$ so the minimum is $\boxed{3}$.

### 22. Prime values
$$n^2-5n+6=(n-2)(n-3).$$ For integer $n$, this product is prime only when one factor is $1$ and the other is prime: $\boxed{n=4}$ gives $2$, and $\boxed{n=1}$ gives $2$.

### 23. Find $xy$ and $x,y$
$$xy=\frac{(x+y)^2-(x^2+y^2)}2=\frac{25-13}{2}=6.$$ Thus $x,y$ are roots of $t^2-5t+6=0$, so $\boxed{\{x,y\}=\{2,3\}}$.

### 24. Solve
Break at $x=-2,1$. The middle interval gives $3=7$, impossible; the outer intervals give $\boxed{x=-4,3}$.

### 25. Solve
$$2(x^2-1)=x^2+1\implies x^2=3,$$ so $\boxed{x=\pm\sqrt3}$.

### 26. Number of roots
Let $u=x^2\ge0$. Then $u^2-4u+1=0$, giving $u=2\pm\sqrt3$, both positive. Each gives two real $x$ values, so there are $\boxed{4}$ roots.

### 27. Find $a,b$
Using roots $1,-2$: $p(1)=a+b+7=0$ and $p(-2)=4a-2b-2=0$. Solving gives $\boxed{a=-1,b=-6}$.

### 28. Largest $xy$
$$xy\le\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)^2=25,$$ with equality at $x=y=5$. The maximum is $\boxed{25}$.

### 29. Solve
Let $u=3^x>0$. Then $u^2-10u+9=(u-1)(u-9)=0$, so $\boxed{x=0,2}$.

### 30. Prove or disprove
Since $(a+b+c)^2\ge3(ab+bc+ca)$, the condition $a+b+c=1$ gives $ab+bc+ca\le\frac13$. Thus the claim $ab+bc+ca\ge\frac13$ is false; equality in the correct inequality occurs at $a=b=c=\frac13$.
