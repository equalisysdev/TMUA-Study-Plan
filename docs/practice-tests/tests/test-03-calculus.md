# TMUA-Type Test 03: Calculus

**Time:** 45 minutes · **Questions:** 10 · Choose one answer per question.

1. The derivative of $x^4-2x$ is: A) 4x^3-2 B) x^3-2 C) 4x^3 D) x^4-2
2. The stationary x-values of $x^3-3x$ are: A) 0 only B) -1,1 C) -3,3 D) 1,3
3. $integral_0^1 2x dx$ equals: A) 0 B) 1/2 C) 1 D) 2
4. The tangent gradient to $y=x^2+1$ at x=3 is: A) 3 B) 5 C) 6 D) 9
5. `integral 1/x dx` is: A) x^-2/2 B) ln|x|+C C) 1/x^2+C D) e^x+C
6. The maximum of $-x^2+6x-2$ is: A) 5 B) 6 C) 7 D) 9
7. If $dy/dx=6x-4$ and y=3 at x=1, y is: A) 3x^2-4x+4 B) 3x^2-4x+3 C) 3x^2-4x D) 6x^2-4x+1
8. The area between y=x and y=x^2 from 0 to 1 is: A) 1/3 B) 1/6 C) 1/2 D) 2/3
9. If f'(x)>0 on an interval, f is: A) decreasing B) constant C) increasing D) quadratic
10. The minimum of `x+9/x` for x>0 is: A) 3 B) 6 C) 9 D) 12
