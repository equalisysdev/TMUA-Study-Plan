# TMUA-Type Test 06: Probability and Mixed Reasoning

**Time:** 45 minutes · **Questions:** 10 · Choose one answer per question.

1. P(exactly 2 heads in 4 fair tosses) is: A) 1/8 B) 1/4 C) 3/8 D) 1/2
2. A bag has 3 red and 2 blue balls. P(red then blue without replacement) is: A) 1/5 B) 3/10 C) 2/5 D) 1/2
3. The expected score of a fair die is: A) 2.5 B) 3 C) 3.5 D) 4
4. Two dice: P(sum at least 10) is: A) 1/12 B) 1/9 C) 1/6 D) 1/4
5. If a statement has a counterexample, it is: A) always true B) not universally true C) necessarily false for all cases D) inductive
6. If $x+y=10$, the minimum of $x^2+y^2$ is: A) 25 B) 50 C) 75 D) 100
7. The roots of $2x^2-8=0$ are: A) 2 only B) -2 only C) -2,2 D) -4,4
8. The derivative of `ln x` is: A) x B) 1/x C) ln x/x D) e^x
9. $\\sin^2 x/(1-\\cos^2 x)$ equals, where defined: A) 0 B) 1 C) \\sin x D) \\cos x
10. The number of distinct arrangements of A,A,B,C is: A) 6 B) 8 C) 12 D) 24
