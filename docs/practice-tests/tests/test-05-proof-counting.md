# TMUA-Type Test 05: Proof and Counting

**Time:** 45 minutes · **Questions:** 10 · Choose one answer per question.

1. A direct proof that an even integer has an even square begins with: A) n=2k B) n=2k+1 C) n=k^2 D) n=0
2. A counterexample disproves a statement claiming: A) existence B) uniqueness C) all cases D) no cases
3. The first step in induction is the: A) contradiction B) base case C) converse D) counterexample
4. The number of arrangements of 4 distinct objects is: A) 4 B) 8 C) 12 D) 24
5. The number of choices of 3 from 7 is: A) 21 B) 28 C) 35 D) 42
6. The coefficient of x^3 in (1+x)^6 is: A) 6 B) 15 C) 20 D) 24
7. The product of consecutive integers n(n+1) is always: A) odd B) even C) prime D) a square
8. The number of paths from (0,0) to (2,2) using right/up steps is: A) 4 B) 5 C) 6 D) 8
9. “If A then B” is false only when: A) A,B true B) A true,B false C) A false,B true D) both false
10. The number of ways to choose a president and secretary from 8 is: A) 16 B) 28 C) 56 D) 64
