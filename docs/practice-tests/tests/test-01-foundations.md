# TMUA-Type Test 01: Foundations

**Time:** 45 minutes · **Questions:** 10 · Choose one answer per question.

1. The roots of `x^2-5x+6=0` are: A) 1,6 B) 2,3 C) -2,-3 D) 0,6
2. If `x+1/x=3`, then `x^2+1/x^2` is: A) 5 B) 7 C) 9 D) 11
3. The domain of `sqrt(2x-6)` is: A) x<3 B) x<=3 C) x>=3 D) x>3
4. The gradient of `y=x^3-3x` at x=2 is: A) 3 B) 6 C) 9 D) 12
5. `sin(pi/6)+cos(pi/3)` equals: A) 0 B) 1/2 C) 1 D) sqrt(3)
6. The number of committees of 2 from 6 people is: A) 12 B) 15 C) 30 D) 36
7. The minimum of `x^2-4x+7` is: A) 2 B) 3 C) 4 D) 7
8. If `f(x)=2x-1`, then `f^-1(5)` is: A) 2 B) 3 C) 5 D) 11
9. A fair die is rolled. P(prime) is: A) 1/6 B) 1/3 C) 1/2 D) 2/3
10. The statement “the product of two odd integers is odd” is best proved by: A) checking 10 cases B) writing each as 2k+1 C) induction only D) contradiction only
