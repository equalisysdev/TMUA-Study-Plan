# Minimum Viable TMUA Routine

Use this on busy days when you cannot do a full plan.

## Core rule
Even a short session is valuable if it is consistent.

## The minimum session
### 1. Lunch block (45–60 minutes)
- 10 minutes: formula recall and quick warm-up
- 20 minutes: one topic review
- 15 minutes: 5–8 short questions
- 5 minutes: log mistakes

### 2. Ride home (20–30 minutes)
- 5 quick formula or method questions
- 2–3 recap items from the topic you studied
- 5-minute error log

## Busy-day template
### Monday
- Algebra or functions review
- 5 quick questions

### Tuesday
- Calculus recap
- derivative/integral review

### Wednesday
- Trigonometry or geometry
- 5 short angle or line questions

### Thursday
- Proof or counting
- one short combinatorics drill

### Friday
- Mixed set or timed section
- mark and review mistakes

## Weekend minimum
- 1 short timed set of 20–30 minutes
- 15–20 minutes of error correction

## If you miss a day
Do not try to catch up with a huge block. Resume with the next planned session.

## The principle
The goal is not to do everything every day. The goal is to keep the habit alive and steadily improve the weak points.
