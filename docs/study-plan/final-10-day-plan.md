# Final 10-Day TMUA Preparation Plan

## Goal
This plan is designed for the last 10 days before the exam and is built around your available study time: lunch breaks and your 30-minute ride home.

## Planning principle
Do not begin new topics in the last 10 days. Focus on:
- method accuracy
- pacing
- quick recall
- review of mistakes
- confidence building

## Daily structure

### Standard day
- Lunch block: 1 hour
- Commute block: 30 minutes

### Daily routine
- 15 minutes: formula recall and warm-up
- 25 minutes: topic revision or method work
- 20 minutes: short mixed set or practice questions
- Ride home: 5–8 quick questions or formula review

## 10-day outline

### Day 1 — Reset and assess
- Light review of algebra and functions
- Do 20 mixed short questions
- Write down recurring weak points

### Day 2 — Calculus precision day
- Differentiation and integration recap
- 25–30 minute timed set
- Review all mistakes thoroughly

### Day 3 — Trigonometry and geometry day
- Identities, graph values, coordinate geometry
- 20 short timed questions
- Mark and correct all errors

### Day 4 — Proof and counting day
- Induction, direct proof, permutations and combinations
- Focus on careful reasoning and avoiding overcounting

### Day 5 — Full timed section
- One 60-minute mixed paper
- Strict timing
- Review every incorrect answer

### Day 6 — Recovery + weak-area repair
- Light revision of your weakest topic
- Short practice session only
- No overloading

### Day 7 — Full timed mock
- Simulate a realistic exam session
- Stick to exact time limits
- Note where the pace lagged

### Day 8 — Exam technique and confidence
- Quick review of formulae and standard methods
- Short problem set without heavy effort
- Focus on calm, fast thinking

### Day 9 — Light tapering
- 60–90 minutes of high-quality review only
- No new difficult material
- Keep the mind fresh and calm

### Day 10 — Exam day preparation
- Light recap of key methods
- Stop heavy revision early
- Sleep well and prepare a calm routine

## Exam-day advice
- Read questions carefully before solving
- Do not get stuck on one difficult question
- Mark omitted questions clearly
- If unsure, use elimination and special-case testing
- Aim for steady accuracy, not perfection on every question

## Weekly objective for this final phase
The target is not to learn new content. It is to make your current methods reliable, fast, and calm under time pressure.

## Final rule
If a session ever feels heavy, reduce to the minimum viable version:
- 1 strong lunch block
- 1 commute review block
- 10 minutes of error log

Consistency wins in the final stretch.
