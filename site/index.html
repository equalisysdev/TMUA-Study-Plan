TMUA Master Study Programme

WARNING: THIS WAS VIBECODED

This workspace contains a structured, exam-focused preparation plan for the Test of Mathematics for University Admission (TMUA), built around a target sitting on January 4th.

Objective

The goal is to build the exact skills needed for TMUA-style problem solving under timed conditions: algebra, functions, calculus, trigonometry, geometry, proof, combinatorics, and mathematical reasoning.

This programme is designed to be: - realistic for a 16-week preparation period - focused on exam-style accuracy and speed - organised into concept learning, drill work, and timed practice - easy to track through revision logs and error logs

Assumed exam format

This plan follows the standard TMUA model used in recent admissions preparation: a timed, multiple-choice mathematics paper with strong emphasis on reasoning, algebraic manipulation, and problem-solving speed. Check the latest official specification before the final sitting, but this programme is aligned to the standard TMUA pattern and difficulty profile.

Folder structure

  • study-plan/ — overall strategy, topic map, and timetable
  • exercises/chapters/ — six organised chapters with 30 exercises each
  • courses/ — six standalone explanatory Markdown courses
  • practice-tests/tests/ — eight timed TMUA-type tests
  • practice-tests/answers/ — separate answer keys and method notes
  • templates/ — revision and error tracking sheets
  • resources.md — Markdown index for explanatory courses and every study resource
  • website/ — interactive version of this study plan

Weekly study rhythm

  • 5 study days per week
  • 1 main lunch block per day plus a short commute review
  • 1 timed paper every 7–10 days
  • 1 review session after each timed paper
  • 1 weak-area recovery session each week

Recommended daily structure

  1. Warm-up: 10 minutes of quick algebra or recall
  2. Concept work: 25–30 minutes
  3. Drill practice: 20–25 minutes
  4. Timed set or mixed review: 10–15 minutes
  5. Error log: 5–10 minutes

This plan is designed around a realistic 1-hour lunch window and a 30-minute ride home.

Core topics covered

  • algebra and manipulation
  • equations and inequalities
  • functions and graphs
  • sequences and series
  • differentiation and integration
  • trigonometry and geometry
  • proofs and logical structure
  • combinatorics and probability
  • problem solving under time pressure

Start here

  1. Read the programme guide in study-plan/tmua-study-program.md
  2. Browse the full resource index in resources.md
  3. Follow the dates in study-plan/week-by-week-timetable.md
  4. Browse the chapter index in exercises/README.md
  5. Run the timed tests from practice-tests/README.md
  6. Open answer keys only after completing each test
  7. Use the templates in templates/ to record progress and mistakes
  8. Open the interactive route in website/index.html

Target date

January 4th

This plan intentionally includes a final revision phase before the exam and a small buffer for recovery, corrections, and confidence-building.
