Detailed Study Timetable for a January 4th TMUA Sitting

Phase overview

This 16-week timetable begins on September 20th and ends on January 4th.

Weekly plan by date

Week 1 — 20 Sep to 26 Sep

Theme: diagnostic + algebra foundations - Baseline timed mini-test - Review algebraic manipulation - Solve 20–25 short algebra questions - Begin function notation and graph reading

Week 2 — 27 Sep to 3 Oct

Theme: functions and equations - Quadratics, factorisation, roots - Simultaneous equations - Graph transformations - Short timed mixed set

Week 3 — 4 Oct to 10 Oct

Theme: inequalities and sequences - Linear and quadratic inequalities - Polynomial division and factorisation - Arithmetic and geometric sequences - 1 full timed batch

Week 4 — 11 Oct to 17 Oct

Theme: calculus basics - Differentiation rules - Stationary points and curve sketching - Tangents and normals - Basic integration

Week 5 — 18 Oct to 24 Oct

Theme: calculus applications - Increasing/decreasing functions - Rate of change - Area and integration - Timed mixed paper

Week 6 — 25 Oct to 31 Oct

Theme: trigonometry and geometry - Trig identities and equations - Unit circle and graph behaviour - Coordinate geometry - Harder geometry reasoning

Week 7 — 1 Nov to 7 Nov

Theme: geometry and proof - Proof by induction - Deductive reasoning - Angle and triangle problems - Review all prior weeks

Week 8 — 8 Nov to 14 Nov

Theme: combinatorics and counting - Permutations and combinations - Binomial expansion - Probability basics - Timed mixed set

Week 9 — 15 Nov to 21 Nov

Theme: probability and logic - Conditional probability - Expected value and simple distributions - Logic puzzles and concise proof - Review mistakes from earlier sets

Week 10 — 22 Nov to 28 Nov

Theme: mixed reasoning - Mixed paper with no topic isolation - Focus on method choice - Timed section practice

Week 11 — 29 Nov to 5 Dec

Theme: stronger exam pacing - 2 timed papers in the week - Review error patterns - Full conceptual drill on weak topics

Week 12 — 6 Dec to 12 Dec

Theme: advanced mixed practice - Harder algebra and calculus questions - Geometry + trig mixed set - Short timed question bursts

Week 13 — 13 Dec to 19 Dec

Theme: mock exam phase 1 - Full mock exam under realistic conditions - Analysis of accuracy and timings - Convert errors into repair tasks

Week 14 — 20 Dec to 26 Dec

Theme: repair and consolidation - Weak-topic recovery - Short but high-quality review sessions - Strategic mixed practice

Week 15 — 27 Dec to 2 Jan

Theme: final pre-exam polishing - Light but intense focused review - Short timed sets - Calm revision, no heavy overload

Week 16 — 3 Jan to 4 Jan

Theme: exam readiness - Light revision with strong confidence work - Final light mock if needed - Sleep, rest, and paper-day routine

Daily time targets

  • Normal day: 2–3 focused study blocks
  • Timed paper day: 2 blocks + full mock simulation
  • Recovery day: 1 light revision block + error log

Recommended weekly mix

  • 3 concept blocks
  • 2 technique blocks
  • 1 mixed review block
  • 1 timed paper block
  • 1 error correction block

Exam week guidance

The final 3–4 days before January 4th should be calm and structured: - revise key methods - avoid starting new topics - keep confidence high - test pacing, not new content
