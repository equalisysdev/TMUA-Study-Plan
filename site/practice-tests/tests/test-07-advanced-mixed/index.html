TMUA-Type Test 07: Advanced Mixed Practice

Time: 60 minutes · Questions: 10 · Choose one answer per question.

  1. If x+1/x=4, then x^2+1/x^2 is: A) 12 B) 14 C) 16 D) 18
  2. The number of real roots of x^4-10x^2+9=0 is: A) 2 B) 3 C) 4 D) 5
  3. If f is strictly increasing, then it is: A) even B) one-to-one C) periodic D) bounded
  4. The maximum of sin x+cos x is: A) 1 B) sqrt(2) C) 2 D) 2sqrt(2)
  5. The stationary points of x^3-12x have x-values: A) -4,4 B) -2,2 C) 0,2 D) 2,4
  6. The number of positive integer solutions to x+y+z=6 is: A) 6 B) 10 C) 15 D) 21
  7. If p|ab and p is prime, the relevant theorem says: A) p|a and p|b B) p|a or p|b C) a|p or b|p D) p=a+b
  8. The area under y=2x from 0 to 3 is: A) 3 B) 6 C) 9 D) 12
  9. The inverse of x/(1+x) is: A) x/(1-x) B) x/(1+x) C) (1-x)/x D) 1/x-1
  10. P(no fixed points in a random permutation of 3 objects) is: A) 1/6 B) 1/3 C) 1/2 D) 2/3
