Week-by-Week TMUA Timetable (20 Sep 2026 to 4 Jan 2027)

This plan is built around your actual available study windows: - 1-hour lunch block - 30-minute ride home - one weekend focused block

The objective is steady progress without overload.

Sunday 20 Sep

  • 20 minutes: set up notes, checklist, and error \log

  • 15 minutes: review weak areas and decide targets

  • Ride home: 5 short algebra questions and error \log

  • Keep the lunch block focused and not overloaded.
