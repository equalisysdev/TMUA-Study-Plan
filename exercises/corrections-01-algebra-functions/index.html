Corrections: Algebra and Functions

Exercise Set A: Algebraic manipulation

1. Simplify

$$ \frac{x^2-9}{x^2-5x+6} =\frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{(x-2)(x-3)} =\frac{x+3}{x-2}. $$

The original expression requires $x\ne 2,3$.

2. Solve

$$ 3x^2-11x+6=(3x-2)(x-3)=0, $$ so

$$ \boxed{x=\frac{2}{3}\text{ or }x=3}. $$

3. Solve for $x$

The restrictions are $x\ne 1$ and $x\ne -2$. Cross-multiplication gives

$$ 2(x+2)=5(x-1), $$ so $2x+4=5x-5$ and therefore

$$ \boxed{x=3}. $$

4. Factor completely

The roots are $1$, $2$, and $3$, so

$$ x^3-6x^2+11x-6=(x-1)(x-2)(x-3). $$

5. Find $x^2+y^2$

Use $(x+y)^2=x^2+2xy+y^2$:

$$ x^2+y^2=7^2-2(10)=49-20=\boxed{29}. $$

Exercise Set B: Inequalities and quadratics

6. Solve

The critical values are $x=2$ and $x=-3$. The product is positive outside the roots:

$$ \boxed{x<-3\text{ or }x>2}. $$

7. Solve

$$ |2x-5|\le 7 \iff -7\le 2x-5\le 7. $$

Adding $5$ and dividing by $2$ gives

$$ \boxed{-1\le x\le 6}. $$

8. Real roots

The discriminant must be non-negative:

$$ (-4)^2-4k\ge 0 \iff 16-4k\ge 0 \iff \boxed{k\le 4}. $$

9. Solve

The critical values are $-1$ and $2$. A sign check shows the fraction is negative between them, excluding $2$:

$$ \boxed{-1<x<2}. $$

10. Vertex and axis

Complete the square:

$$ f(x)=x^2-4x+3=(x-2)^2-1. $$

Therefore the vertex is $\boxed{(2,-1)}$ and the axis of symmetry is $\boxed{x=2}$.

Exercise Set C: Functions and graph transformations

11. Sketch and minimum

The graph is $y=x^2$ shifted right by $1$ and up by $2$. Its vertex is $(1,2)$, so the minimum value is

$$ \boxed{2}. $$

12. Describe the transformation

From $y=x^2$ to $y=-2(x+3)^2+1$:

  1. shift left by $3$;
  2. stretch vertically by factor $2$;
  3. reflect in the $x$-axis;
  4. shift up by $1$.

13. Find the inverse

Let $y=3x-5$. Swap $x$ and $y$ and solve:

$$ x=3y-5\implies y=\frac{x+5}{3}. $$

Thus

$$ \boxed{g^{-1}(x)=\frac{x+5}{3}}. $$

14. Find the domain

For the square root to be defined,

$$ x^2-9\ge 0 \iff (x-3)(x+3)\ge 0. $$

Hence

$$ \boxed{x\le -3\text{ or }x\ge 3}. $$

15. Find $f(g(x))$

Substitute $g(x)=x+2$ into $f$:

$$ f(g(x))=(x+2)^2-1=x^2+4x+3. $$

Therefore $\boxed{f(g(x))=x^2+4x+3}$.
