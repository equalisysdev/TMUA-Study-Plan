Corrections: Trigonometry and Geometry

Exercise Set A: Trigonometric identities

1. Simplify

$$ \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}=\boxed{\tan x}, $$ provided $\cos x\ne 0$.

2. Prove the identity

On the unit circle, the coordinates of an angle $x$ are $(\cos x,\sin x)$. The radius is $1$, so

$$ (\cos x)^2+(\sin x)^2=1. $$

Therefore $\boxed{\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1}$.

3. Solve for $x\in[0,2\pi]$

The sine value $1/2$ occurs at angles $\pi/6$ and $5\pi/6$ in this interval:

$$ \boxed{x=\frac{\pi}{6}\text{ or }x=\frac{5\pi}{6}}. $$

4. Solve

$$ 2\cos x-1=0\implies \cos x=\frac12. $$

For one full turn,

$$ \boxed{x=\frac{\pi}{3}+2k\pi\text{ or }x=\frac{5\pi}{3}+2k\pi}, \qquad k\in\mathbb Z. $$

5. Simplify

Using $1-\cos^2x=\sin^2x$,

$$ \frac{1-\cos^2x}{\sin x}=\frac{\sin^2x}{\sin x}=\boxed{\sin x}, $$ provided $\sin x\ne 0$.

Exercise Set B: Geometry and coordinate work

6. Midpoint

$$ \left(\frac{2+8}{2},\frac{5+1}{2}\right)=\boxed{(5,3)}. $$

7. Equation of the line

Use $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$ with $(x_1,y_1)=(1,3)$ and $m=2$:

$$ y-3=2(x-1), $$ so

$$ \boxed{y=2x+1}. $$

8. Distance

$$ PQ=\sqrt{(5-(-1))^2+(10-2)^2} =\sqrt{36+64} =\boxed{10}. $$

9. Intersection points

Set the equations equal:

$$ x^2=3x-2\implies x^2-3x+2=0 \implies (x-1)(x-2)=0. $$

The corresponding points are

$$ \boxed{(1,1)\text{ and }(2,4)}. $$

10. Area of the triangle

Drop a perpendicular from the apex to the base of length $8$. It bisects the base into lengths $4$ and $4$. The height is

$$ h=\sqrt{5^2-4^2}=3. $$

Therefore

$$ \text{area}=\frac12(8)(3)=\boxed{12}. $$

Exercise Set C: Reasoning problems

11. Equation of the line

The gradient is

$$ m=\frac{9-1}{4-0}=2. $$

Since the line passes through $(0,1)$, its intercept is $1$. Hence

$$ \boxed{y=2x+1}. $$

12. Angle with the $x$-axis

The line $y=x$ has gradient $1$. Since $\tan\theta=1$,

$$ \boxed{\theta=\frac{\pi}{4}=45^\circ}. $$

13. Shorter leg

In a $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle, the side opposite $30^\circ$ is half the hypotenuse:

$$ \boxed{5}. $$

14. Simplify

Using the cofunction identity,

$$ \boxed{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right)=\cos x}. $$

15. Circle equation

The radius is the distance from $(0,0)$ to $(3,4)$:

$$ r=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\boxed{5}. $$

Thus the circle is

$$ \boxed{x^2+y^2=25}. $$
