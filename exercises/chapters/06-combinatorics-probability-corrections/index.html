Corrections: Combinatorics and Probability

Foundation

1. Books

Order matters, so $5!=\boxed{120}$.

2. Committees

Order does not matter: $\binom83=\boxed{56}$.

3. Three-digit numbers

Choose and arrange three of five digits: $5\cdot4\cdot3=\boxed{60}$.

4. Two dice

There are $6\cdot6=\boxed{36}$ ordered outcomes.

5. Roll a 6

$$\boxed{P(6)=\frac16}.$$

6. Exactly two heads

$$\binom32\left(\frac12\right)^3=\boxed{\frac38}.$$

7. Total of 7

There are six favourable ordered pairs out of $36$, so $\boxed{1/6}$.

8. Subsets

Each element is either included or excluded: $2^5=\boxed{32}$.

9. Coefficient

The coefficient is $\binom52=\boxed{10}$.

10. Captain and vice-captain

Roles differ, so $10\cdot9=\boxed{90}$.

Core technique

11. MATH arrangements

All letters differ, so $4!=\boxed{24}$.

12. A before B

Exactly half of the $4!$ arrangements have A before B: $\boxed{12}$.

13. Four-digit even numbers

The final digit is $2$ or $4$ (2 choices), then arrange three of the remaining four digits: $2\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2=\boxed{48}$.

14. Two red cards

$$P=\frac{26}{52}\cdot\frac{25}{51}=\boxed{\frac5{51}}.$$

15. Different colours

$$P=\frac4{10}\frac6{9}+\frac6{10}\frac4{9}=\boxed{\frac8{15}}.$$

16. Second score greater

There are $15$ ordered pairs with second score greater, out of $36$: $\boxed{5/12}$.

17. Expected die score

$$E=\frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6}=\boxed{\frac72}.$$

18. Coefficient

Choose three $x$ terms and two $2$ terms: $$\binom53 2^2=\boxed{40}.$$

19. Nonnegative solutions

By stars and bars, $\binom{7+3-1}{3-1}=\binom92=\boxed{36}$.

20. Grid paths

There are three right and two up steps, so choose the positions of the two up steps: $\binom52=\boxed{10}$.

TMUA challenge

21. BANANA

There are six letters, with A repeated three times and N twice: $$\frac{6!}{3!2!}=\boxed{60}.$$

22. Committee restrictions

The two specified people are included and the third excluded. Choose the remaining two from seven: $\binom72=\boxed{21}$.

23. Exactly two girls

$$\binom42\left(\frac12\right)^4=\boxed{\frac38}.$$

24. Exactly two red

$$P=\frac{\binom52\binom41}{\binom93}=\frac{40}{84}=\boxed{\frac{10}{21}}.$$

25. Product a multiple of 6

Count ordered pairs with a factor $2$ and a factor $3$. There are $15$ favourable outcomes out of $36$, so $\boxed{5/12}$.

26. Expected heads

By linearity of expectation, $8\cdot\frac12=\boxed4$.

27. Positive solutions

Set $a=x-1,b=y-1,c=z-1$. Then $a+b+c=7$, giving $\binom92=\boxed{36}$ solutions.

28. Circular arrangements

Rotations are equivalent, so fix one person and arrange the rest: $5!=\boxed{120}$.

29. Exactly two A answers

Choose the two positions containing A, and fill the other six with any of three non-A choices: $$\binom82 3^6=\boxed{20412}.$$

30. Derangement probability

The number of derangements of five objects is $$!5=5!\left(1-1+\frac12-\frac16+\frac1{24}-\frac1{120}\right)=44.$$ Thus the probability is $44/5!=\boxed{11/30}$.
