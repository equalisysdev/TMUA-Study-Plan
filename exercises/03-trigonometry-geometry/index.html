Trigonometry and Geometry Drills

Learning objectives

  • solve trig equations and use identities confidently
  • handle coordinate geometry and line/curve problems
  • reason with geometry and supplementary angle facts
  • avoid algebraic slips in shaped problems

Exercise Set A: Trigonometric identities

1. Simplify

$$ $frac{\$\sin x}{\\cos x} $$

2. Prove

$$ $\sin^2 x + \$\cos^2 x = 1 $$

3. Solve for $x$in[0,2\$\pi]$

$$ $\sin x = \$frac{1}{2} $$

4. Solve

$$ 2\\cos x - 1 = 0 $$

5. Simplify

$$ $frac{1-\$\cos^2 x}{\\sin x} $$

Exercise Set B: Geometry and coordinate work

6. Find the midpoint of the segment joining $$A(2,5)$ and \$$B(8,1)$.

7. Find the equation of the line through \$ (1,3) $ with slope 2.

8. Find the distance between $$P(-1,2)$ and \$$Q(5,10)$.

9. The line $y=3x-2$ intersects the curve $y=x^2$. Find the intersection points.

10. A triangle has sides 5, 5, and 8. Find its area.

Exercise Set C: Reasoning problems

11. Show that the line joining points $$A(0,1)$ and \$$B(4,9)$ has equation $y=2x+1$.

12. Find the angle between the line $y=x$ and the x-axis.

13. A right triangle has one acute angle of 30°. If the hypotenuse is 10, find the shorter leg.

14. Simplify

$$ \\sin(90^ -x) $$

15. The point $(3,4)$ lies on a circle centered at the origin. Find the radius and the equation of the circle.

Technique focus

  • Learn common identities and rearrangements
  • Always consider general solutions and principal ranges
  • For geometry, draw a diagram before solving
  • Use coordinate formulas efficiently

Answer notes

  • Question 1: recognise tangent identity
  • Question 3: know standard sine values on the unit circle
  • Question 4: solve cosine equation by isolating \cos x
  • Question 10: Heron’s formula or isosceles triangle area method
  • Questions 6–9: use formulas repeatedly without friction
  • Questions 12–15: practise angle and coordinate reasoning
